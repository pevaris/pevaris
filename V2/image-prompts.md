# Pevaris Website — Image Briefs for Claude Design

Use these briefs to generate custom images for the Pevaris website. All images should feel premium, modern, and aligned with a high-end technology consultancy. Colour palette: deep evergreen (#14281D), bone/cream (#E7DFD0), hunter green (#355834), and gold accents (#B9A563).

---

## 1. Homepage — "Technology that isn't delivering"
**File:** `index.html`
**Section:** The Problem
**Current alt text:** "Technology strategy discussion"

**Context:** This section addresses businesses whose technology investments aren't producing commercial results — strategy documents gathering dust, AI projects stuck in proof-of-concept.

**Brief:**
A thoughtful, slightly abstracted scene suggesting stalled progress or disconnect. Perhaps a boardroom table with strategy documents and a laptop, but the people are absent or out of focus — conveying that plans exist but execution is missing. Muted, professional tones. No obvious frustration — more quiet tension. Could also be a single figure looking at complex dashboards or charts that feel overwhelming or disconnected from reality.

## Style Notes for All Images

- **Colour palette:** Deep evergreen (#14281D), bone/cream (#E7DFD0), hunter green (#355834), gold (#B9A563)
- **Mood:** Premium, confident, understated. Not flashy or startup-y.
- **People:** When shown, diverse, professional, engaged. Senior presence preferred.
- **Lighting:** Natural, warm, modern. Avoid harsh corporate fluorescent feels.
- **Composition:** Clean, uncluttered. Negative space is good.
- **Avoid:** Stock photo clichés (handshakes, pointing at screens, fake smiles), sci-fi AI imagery, chaotic/messy workspaces
