AI Strategy

AI that actually works.

Most AI projects fail not because of the technology — but because the strategy was disconnected from the commercial reality. I start with the business problem, not the model.

The reality

Why most AI programmes stall.

01
Strategy built around the tech, not the business

Teams get excited about models and tools before anyone has defined what commercial problem they're solving. The result is impressive demos that never reach production.

02
Governance bolted on as an afterthought

Compliance, data privacy, and risk management get added late — slowing deployment, creating legal exposure, and undermining board confidence.

03
No clear definition of success

Without commercial KPIs defined upfront, AI programmes get measured in model accuracy instead of revenue impact — and struggle to justify continued investment.

My approach

Commercial-first AI strategy.

Commercial diagnosis

Identify where AI creates measurable commercial value — cost reduction, revenue growth, or competitive differentiation.

Readiness & data audit

Assess your data infrastructure, team capability, and organisational readiness — so the strategy is grounded in reality.

Governance framework

Compliance, risk, and responsible AI policies designed for your sector — built in from the start, not retrofitted.

Production & measurement

Deployment with the delivery capability to match — and KPIs tied to revenue, not model performance metrics.

Where AI creates value

Common use cases across sectors.

Automation
Intelligent process automation

Eliminating manual, high-volume processes that drain resource without adding commercial value — from document processing to operational workflows.

Customer
Revenue intelligence

Using AI to identify expansion revenue, churn risk, and upsell opportunity across your customer base — with commercial interventions that actually work.

Operations
Decision augmentation

Giving your leadership and operational teams better, faster information at the point of decision — reducing cost and improving outcomes simultaneously.

Product

Compliance
